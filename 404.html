The team app for check-in, hours, tasks, and calendar.

NEO is the internal employee app used by Cartel PR, Sharks Agency, Gray, and JBnJaws. Team members sign in with Google to track attendance, manage tasks, and keep their calendar in sync.

  • Check-in & hours
  • Tasks
  • Google Calendar
  • Team directory

Who it is for

Invited employees of Cartel PR and sister companies. Access is by invite only.

How you sign in

Google sign-in is the only sign-in method. There are no passwords stored in NEO.

What it does

Check in and out, log hours, work through tasks, and view or create calendar events with teammates.

Privacy

Read how NEO uses Google, location, and calendar data on the Privacy Policy.
